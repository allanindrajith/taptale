Photos were only downscaled (max 1600 px wide) and recompressed for the app; no other changes were made. Attribution is shown in the app on each spot page and in the photo credits sheet (`src/components/photo-credits.tsx`); machine-readable data lives in `src/constants/photo-credits.ts`.
